Research

We build technologies that reimagine how people create, consume, and interact with media.

01

Computational Media Generation & Editing

We develop algorithms and systems that automate and enhance media production — from stage video cross-editing to highlight generation and virtual cinematography.

02

Immersive User Experience

We design and optimize how users experience media — including subtitle presentation, comment display, and personalized playback — across diverse devices and contexts.

03

Human Interaction & Performance

We study how people interact with systems — from precise temporal input to AI response latency — to model and improve human performance in digital environments.
